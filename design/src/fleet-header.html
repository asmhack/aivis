Sticky over the index: identity, connection state, and the counts the fleet reduces to. Three of them are states a session is in — working, idle, parked — and the other three are queues drawn from those sessions, so waiting on you is a subset of idle rather than a rival to it, and asking you a subset of working. Idle earns a count of its own because it is the state most of a busy machine is in: a terminal left open costs nothing, and a header that omitted it read as an empty machine. The ? at the end opens the legend, which is where those words are actually defined. Throughput and the block meter sit on the right, where a figure that only ever changes slowly belongs.
