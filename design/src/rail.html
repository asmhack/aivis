Session rail · workflows

The rail opens at half the window width and is drag-resizable from its left edge. Workflows are navigated one level at a time — run, phase, task, agent — so a 74-agent run never renders as a wall of chips. Every level carries its own progress and spend; failures are pulled to the top of the level they occurred in. Opening an agent never replaces the level: the rail widens to three quarters of the window and the agent docks beside the phase’s full agent list, so moving to the next agent is one click rather than a trip back up.
